#4471 was three problems, not one

owner support desk proposed reviewed 2026-10-29 · 0 days ago

#4471 merged 412 reports into one "known" issue: the parser reads dates month first, tenants write day first. That rule comes from decision 0001: a report is a duplicate when its subject matches an open ticket's. Nobody has to read the body to merge on subject. Nobody did.

Reading the bodies and running causes.ts against the real tenant data shows #4471 is not one bug. It is three.

Same subject line, different bug. The desk's own dedupe rule is what hid this.

The three problems

#4471 holds 412 unique reports (427 rows in the export, 15 of them re-sent across overlapping export pages). causes.ts's own groupByCause splits them 379 / 33 by cause. A third problem sits inside that 379, found only by reading bodies, not counted by causes.ts at all.

problemreportstenantswhat is actually happening
parser reads month-first 379 (desk's own cause split) all 11 Every tenant here writes day-first dates. The shared parser always reads MM/DD, so app, email, and booking-v2-form submissions come out with month and day swapped.
booking-v3 hint mismatches locale 33 (desk's own cause split) alder.physio, copper.studio, pale.harbour The booking-v3 form prints a MM/DD/YYYY hint to tenants whose locale is day-first. People type what the hint says; the form then parses it the tenant's (day-first) way. Same symptom as the row above, different cause, a different fix.
one-day shift, not a swap 5 (inside the 379 above, not separate from it) kettle.books, alder.physio, nine.lanterns, pale.harbour, lantern.row Reports like "12 May showed as 11 May" and "Lisbon got Tuesday, Toronto got Monday, same meeting" are off by exactly one day, same month. A month/day swap does not produce that. causes.ts has no category for this, so groupByCause counts these 5 as parser anyway. They are a third, unrelated bug, not a parser miss.

How the first two were found

causes.ts already splits parser from hint by tenant locale and form (groupByCause). Its CauseTable component renders both rows for #4471 today, and marks whichever one isn't the ticket's main cause as "not this ticket — no ticket of its own". That table was the start: the split was already computed, just never given a ticket.

How the third was found

causes.ts has no notion of a one-day shift — it only tells parser from hint. The desk's own desk-notes.json carries an oneDayMove list of five report ids, read differently by whoever triaged them, but only ever shown as a footnote under whichever cause they happened to land in. Reading those five bodies: none of them describe a swapped month and day. All of them describe a date that is one day off, in the same month. They were merged into #4471 because their subject lines matched, not because they are the same bug:

  • R-41202 (kettle.books, email) — invite said Tuesday 12 May, calendar said Monday 11 May.
  • R-41209 (alder.physio, app) — "Every booking in June is one day off. Only June."
  • R-41236 (nine.lanterns, app) — "The invite arrived before I made the booking."
  • R-41344 (pale.harbour, email) — same meeting, Tuesday in Lisbon, Monday in Toronto.
  • R-41620 (lantern.row, email) — "Tuesday 6 October showed as Monday. I moved it myself."

Lessons

  • Subject-line dedupe (decision 0001) merges fast and merges wrong: it can put two different bugs in one ticket as easily as it splits one bug into two.
  • A table that already shows a second cause (CauseTable, on #4471 today) is a problem without a ticket, not a curiosity.
  • A footnote (desk-notes.json's oneDayMove) is where a third bug hides when nobody reads the bodies behind a "known" ticket.
